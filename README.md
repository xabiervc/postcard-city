# Postcard City

## Status
Preimplementation foundation.

## Vision
Postcard City is a political regional city simulator about tourism, housing, employment, public services, economic diversification, power, media, and livability.

Each run presents a different region with a distinct history, initial condition, political landscape, opportunities, and crises. The player governs it through competing pressures and decides whether it becomes a thriving place to live, a resilient mixed economy, or a profitable postcard that no longer belongs to its residents.

## Design north star
Turn a region into a destination without making it impossible to live there.

## Repository structure
- `docs/`: product and design documentation.
- `schemas/`: machine-readable content contracts.
- `config/`: design and prototype configuration.
- `tests/`: future automated tests.
- `tools/`: future authoring and validation tools.
- `data/`: future authored and generated content.

## Current milestone
Preimplementation: lock the product definition, simulation boundaries, vertical slice, content contracts, technical risks, and quality gates before full production.

## Language policy
Repository documentation and content schemas are written in English for consistency across the development workflow and LLM-assisted tooling. Player-facing localization is specified separately.
