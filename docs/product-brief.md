# Product Brief

## Working title
Postcard City

## Product definition
A political regional city simulator in which every run begins with a different urban condition, power structure, opportunity set, and crisis profile. The player governs through several mandates and balances tourism, housing, employment, public services, diversification, institutional trust, media pressure, opposition, and private investment.

## Player promise
Build a destination without destroying the place people call home.

## Target experience
The player should feel like a consequential regional leader making difficult decisions with incomplete information. The city must visibly and statistically change in response to policy.

## Differentiation
Postcard City is not a pure sandbox city builder and not a literal theme-park manager. Construction is a policy instrument. Tourism is the central transforming force, while housing, work, services, industry, media, and elections expose its consequences.

## Core audience
- Strategy and management players.
- Players interested in political and social simulation.
- Players who enjoy emergent narrative.
- Players who prefer meaningful trade-offs over optimal building puzzles.
- Players who want adjustable complexity.

## Modes
- Campaign: authored arcs introducing the systems progressively.
- Scenario: generated or authored regional problems with clear objectives.
- Sandbox: indefinite governance with configurable systems and periodic evaluation.
- Custom: per-system complexity and rule configuration.

## Product pillars
1. A living region, not an empty grid.
2. Tourism as opportunity and pressure.
3. Every solution creates second-order effects.
4. Power includes citizens, business, institutions, media, and opposition.
5. Procedural variation creates coherent cases, not random noise.
6. Complexity is scalable and explainable.
7. The city tells the story visually.

## Scope guardrails
The first playable milestone focuses on one region, one tourism crisis, one major investment proposal, housing, transport, healthcare staffing, basic elections, media, opposition, and a complete evaluation screen. Detailed crime, terrorism, deep industry chains, and advanced corruption are deferred until the core loop is proven.

## Success criteria
The vertical slice succeeds if players can explain why the region changed, identify at least two viable strategies, understand the cost of their decisions, and want to replay with a different approach.
