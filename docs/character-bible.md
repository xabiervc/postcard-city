# Character Bible

## Purpose

Characters give human perspective to systemic change. They are not a replacement for the simulation and they do not need full role-playing-game progression.

## Recurring perspectives

| Character | Perspective | Wants | Fears | System link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mara Soler | Resident and caregiver | Stable housing and nearby services | Displacement and loss of community | Rent, migration, social housing |
| Ilias Haddad | Recent migrant and hospitality worker | Safety, work, and belonging | Administrative exclusion | Migration, tourism, services |
| Joana Vidal | Public healthcare worker | Staff, time, and reliable transport | Exhaustion and service collapse | Healthcare, mobility, tourism pressure |
| Tomás Riu | Small local merchant | A viable business without losing local customers | Chain displacement and volatility | Tourism, rents, economic diversity |
| Núria Esteve | Community organizer | Voice for affected residents | Consultation without power | Mobilization, trust, legitimacy |
| Leire Campos | Housing cooperative advocate | Permanently affordable homes | Token projects and financial fragility | Social housing, budget, arrears |
| Bruno Keller | Tourism and development representative | Investment and visitor growth | Regulation that destroys demand | Land, tourism, project profile |
| Ada Ferrer | Civic journalist and archivist | Verifiable public memory | Official narratives hiding costs | Evidence, traces, postcards |

## Writing constraints

- Every major intervention should be reflected through at least one relevant perspective.
- A character may disagree with the player's goal without being a villain.
- Testimony must identify what the system changes in lived terms.
- Characters retain memory of major decisions.
- No single character is the authoritative voice of the city.

## Vertical-slice cast

Use Mara, Joana, Núria, and Ada for the first playable slice. This set covers housing, services, mobilization, evidence, and memory without exceeding the complexity budget.
