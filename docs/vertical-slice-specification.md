# Vertical Slice Specification

## Slice title
The Overheated Destination

## Purpose
Prove that Postcard City is a political regional simulation rather than a generic builder. The slice must show a complete decision, consequence, political, media, and evaluation loop.

## Starting region
A coastal historic metropolitan region with a profitable tourist centre, rising housing pressure, a car-dependent outer district, and an understaffed public hospital.

## Initial conditions
- Tourism is growing rapidly.
- Short-term rental conversion is accelerating.
- Permanent residents are leaving the historic centre.
- Healthcare workers live far from the hospital.
- Transport is congested but repairable.
- Public finances are stable but limited.
- The governing coalition is fragile.
- A local investigative outlet has moderate credibility.

## Main opportunity
Aurora Leisure proposes a large integrated hotel and entertainment district. The public proposal promises jobs, visitors, tax income, and transport investment. Hidden uncertainty concerns financing, workforce quality, public infrastructure cost, and requested legal exceptions.

## Player options
- Approve.
- Reject.
- Renegotiate with worker housing and public safeguards.
- Delay for an independent audit.
- Hold a public consultation.
- Approve a smaller alternative.

## Supporting systems
- Housing conversion and affordability.
- Worker accessibility.
- Hospital staffing.
- Public transport.
- Tourism revenue and saturation.
- Basic media funding.
- Opposition response.
- One potential whistleblower.
- One election.

## Slice outcomes
- Tourist boom with service collapse.
- Balanced project with slower growth.
- Rejected project and alternative diversification.
- Political scandal and electoral defeat.
- Transparent governance and improved institutional trust.

## Acceptance criteria
A tester can:
1. Understand the initial diagnosis in under five minutes.
2. Identify at least three relevant trade-offs.
3. Make a decision without needing hidden spreadsheet knowledge.
4. See meaningful changes in at least two districts.
5. Trace at least one outcome to its causes.
6. Experience a credible political or media reaction.
7. Reach at least two distinct viable outcomes.
8. Replay the slice and choose a different strategy.

## Explicit exclusions
No full procedural map generation, deep crime simulation, terrorism simulation, detailed industrial supply chains, full corruption network, or final art pipeline in this slice.
